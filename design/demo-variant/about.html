How we bake

Long fermentation, regional grain and a small team that has been doing this together for a decade.

Our doughs rest between 24 and 36 hours before they see the oven. That is slow by industry standards, and it is the whole point: time does the work that additives would otherwise do. The crust gets darker, the crumb gets more open, and the bread keeps for days.

Grain

Rye and wheat come from Gut Kerkow in the Uckermark and from the Spreewald mill in Lübbenau. We buy whole grain and mill part of it ourselves, fresh, on the morning it is used.

The starter

There are two: a stiff rye starter from 2014 and a liquid wheat starter that is a little younger. Both are fed twice a day. If you want to take some home, ask at the counter — we hand it out on Saturdays.

The range

  • Berliner Landbrot

    Our daily rye-wheat loaf. Dense, sour, lasts a week. 1 kg or 500 g.

  • Dinkel-Saaten

    Spelt with sunflower, flax and pumpkin seeds. Mild and soft; a favourite with children.

  • Pain de Campagne

    Wheat sourdough with a little rye, baked dark. Friday and Saturday only.

  • Weekly special

    Changes every Tuesday. Recently: walnut rye, olive ciabatta, buckwheat loaf.
