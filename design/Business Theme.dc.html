Nera theme · Business · demo: Brot & Zeit, Berlin

Crust, flour and a hot oven.

Cream page, wheat cards, and two dark "oven" bands — hero and footer are set in the text colour with the page colour as ink, so they stay AA under any owner's tokens. A big serif voice (Fraunces, self-hosted; shown here in its Palatino/Georgia fallback), a burnt-crust accent as a thick rule under the header and section titles, a slightly tilted hero photo. Every mockup is the real CSS rendered live — placeholder photos are generated stand-ins; drop in real ones and the layout stays.

Notes

Files: theme.css (tokens only, no hard-coded colours) · tokens.yaml · tokens-variant.yaml · live pages in demo/ and demo-variant/.

Heading font: the default names Fraunces (OFL, one self-hosted file) — what you see is the system fallback (Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia), so the design is proven without it. The dark bands use only color.text and color.bg swapped, so their contrast is the already-checked text/bg pair. Relationship to "Blog": same calm measure and hairline logic, but the Business theme is warmer, louder in type and carries two dark bands.

Phone frames are 390px wide and scrollable; the desktop frames are 1280px. Frames are cropped at a fixed height so the canvas stays scannable.
