Bread that takes its time.

Sourdough, rye and spelt, baked every morning in Prenzlauer Berg. No shortcuts, no additives — flour, water, salt and about 36 hours.

Order for pickupThis week's breads

A neighbourhood bakery since 2014

We started with one oven and a rye starter that is still alive today. Everything is mixed, shaped and baked on Kollwitzstraße — you can watch from the counter while your coffee is poured.

Most of our flour comes from two mills in Brandenburg, less than 80 km away. Read how we bake.

What we do

  • Breads

    Sourdough, rye, spelt and a weekly special. Baked fresh from 7 am; the last loaves usually go out around eleven.

  • Pastries & cakes

    Butter croissants, cardamom buns and one seasonal cake. Whole cakes to order with two days’ notice.

  • Baking courses

    Learn to handle sourdough in a Saturday workshop. Small groups, flour included, a loaf to take home.
