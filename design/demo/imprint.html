Privacy

This site is static. It sets no cookies, loads nothing from third parties and does not track visitors. The only personal data we process is what you send us through the contact form.

Contact form

When you use the form, we store the data you enter in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. We delete it once your request is dealt with, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Your rights

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification or erasure (Art. 16, 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 18, 20 GDPR)
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Berlin the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit

Hosting

The site is hosted in Germany. The hosting provider stores server log files (IP address, time, requested page) for seven days for security purposes.

We would rather bake bread than collect data.
